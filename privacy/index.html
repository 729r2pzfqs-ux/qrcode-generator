Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Your QR codes stay on your device

QRCodes.win generates all QR codes directly in your browser using JavaScript. The text, URLs, WiFi passwords, and contact details you enter are never sent to our servers, never stored, and never shared. When you close the page, the data is gone.

No accounts, no personal data

You do not need to sign up or provide any personal information to use this site. We do not collect names, email addresses, or payment details.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage — which pages are visited, from which countries, and on which device types. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes your IP address on Google’s servers. This data is anonymous and aggregated; we cannot identify you from it. See Google’s Privacy Policy for details.

Cookies

The only cookies on this site are those set by Google Analytics for anonymous usage measurement. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies of our own.

Third-party content

Some fonts and scripts are loaded from public CDNs (Google Fonts, Tailwind CSS, unpkg). These services may see your IP address when your browser requests the files, as with any website.

Your data is never sold

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@qrcodes.win.
